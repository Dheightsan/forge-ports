Request a Minecraft mod port

Is there a mod you want on your version and nobody has ported it? Suggest it on my Discord. People vote on what they want most, and every new port I make gets posted there first.

Join the Discord

How a request works

  1. Post the mod in #pide-un-port: its name or link, the version and loader it is on, and where you want it.
  2. Others vote on it with a thumbs up, so the mods many people want rise to the top.
  3. Before anything gets ported I check its licence and what its author says about ports. Some mods can't be ported, and suggesting one is not the same as ordering it.

The server

It is a small server, around 74 people in November 2026, where I post every port, answer questions and take bug reports for my ports. If a port of mine breaks, that is the place to send the log.

Need it for your own pack now?

A vote takes time. If you need a mod on your version for a pack or a server and can't wait, I can do it as paid work: I check the licence first and tell you a fixed price before you pay anything.
