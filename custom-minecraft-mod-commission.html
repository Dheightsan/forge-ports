Custom Minecraft mods made for your pack

If you want a mob, an item, a block or a mechanic that no mod has, I can make it for your pack on Forge 1.20.1 or NeoForge 1.21.1. Tell me the idea in a few lines. A drawing helps a lot if you have one.

Set up your ticket Join the Discord

What I can make

  • Mobs are what I've done the most. A new animal or enemy, with its model, animations and the biomes where it spawns.
  • Items and blocks, with texture and recipe.
  • Mechanics too, like the herder in Biome Herders that takes the herd out at dawn and brings it back at dusk.

How it goes

  1. You open a ticket from #pedidos on my Discord and tell me the idea, with a drawing or a reference picture if you have one.
  2. I ask what's missing and give you the price there. You don't pay anything before that.
  3. I make it and test it in game. On bigger mods I send screenshots or a short video while I work on it.
  4. You try it in your own pack, and the last payment comes after that.

Price

There's no price list for this. I quote each mod in the ticket, once I know what you want.

What I've made so far

Seven of my own mods are already on CurseForge, all of them mobs:

  • MooffaloA buffalo cow for savannas and taigas. It grazes, can be sheared and lies down next to your crops to help them grow.
  • Barnyard CousinsA lop-eared pig and a dappled cow next to the vanilla ones, with seven coats each that depend on the biome.
  • Biome HerdersVillages get a herder with a sheepdog that takes the herd out to graze at dawn and brings it back at dusk.
  • BeastmastersPillagers show up riding war bears and gators. Kill the rider and you can tame the beast.
  • Heritage HensChickens with genes. Breed them, collect the egg colors, and about 1 in 40 chicks comes out golden.
  • FalconersIllager falconers send a falcon to steal the item in your hand. Kill the falcon or the falconer to get it back.
  • Fruit BatAn orange fruit bat for jungles. It bites you in the dark unless you hold a fruit, and once fed it helps your crops.
